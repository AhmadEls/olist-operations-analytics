# Results & Business Recommendations

## Summary of Findings

The analysis shows that while the majority of deliveries meet SLA commitments, a non-trivial percentage of orders are delivered late. Additionally, the average delay for late orders suggests that SLA breaches are operationally significant rather than minor timing deviations.

---

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Identify High-Risk Sellers
Focus on sellers with consistently higher late delivery rates.  
Introduce performance monitoring and SLA accountability mechanisms.

### 2. Regional Performance Monitoring
Analyze regional SLA performance to detect logistics bottlenecks.  
Investigate fulfillment hubs or last-mile carrier delays in underperforming areas.

### 3. Track Delay Severity, Not Just Volume
Monitoring average days late provides deeper insight than late count alone.  
Severe delays may indicate systemic supply chain issues.

### 4. Introduce SLA Target Benchmarking
Define a formal SLA target (e.g., 95% on-time rate) and monitor variance over time.

### 5. Implement Monthly Trend Tracking
Tracking month-over-month performance helps detect operational improvements or regressions early.

---

## Strategic Impact

A structured SLA monitoring system enables:

- Improved operational visibility  
- Better seller performance management  
- Reduced customer dissatisfaction  
- Data-driven logistics optimization  

This transforms SLA reporting from reactive analysis into proactive operational management.
